Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

What does the free assessment involve?

A consultant reviews your income, expenses and debts and explains which options, if any, suit you. There is no obligation.

What is the difference between mediation and debt review?

Mediation is informal and covers unsecured debt. Debt review is formal under the National Credit Act.

Will this affect my credit record?

Debt review appears on your credit profile and you generally cannot take new credit while it lasts.

Can I include my home loan or car finance?

Not in mediation. Debt review may include them, subject to the process and legal requirements.
