Debt review

A formal process under the National Credit Act for people who are over-indebted or at risk of becoming so.

What you get

  • One structured repayment plan for included accounts.
  • Confirmation by a court or the National Consumer Tribunal.
  • Protection for eligible assets such as a home, vehicle and furniture, subject to legal requirements.
  • No limit on the amount of debt considered.

What to expect

Credit providers and credit bureaus are notified and your credit profile shows you are under debt review. You generally cannot take new credit, all eligible accounts must be included, and the repayment period may be longer.
